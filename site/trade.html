Trade analyser Values Model lab

Link your Sleeper league to trade from real rosters, or add players and picks by hand. Each manager judges the trade on their own horizon and settings, so a contender and a rebuilder can both come out ahead.

How to use it
  • Link your league. Type your Sleeper username, or tap the league's button. Your team goes on side A; choose who you're trading with on side B.
  • Build the trade. Each side lists what it could get from the other team: tap players and picks to add them, or search. You can add FAAB too.
  • Horizon. Each manager is judged on their own horizon: Redraft (this season only), Win Now, Balanced or Long-Term. It starts from where the commissioner has each team (contending, building or rebuilding); move it if you see it differently.
  • Sliders start from each manager's JADL trade history, such as how much they like picks or rookies. Change them under each side.
  • Verdict. The trade from each side's point of view, then the same trade at every horizon. A contender and a rebuilder can both come out ahead.
  • Balancer. If one side loses, it suggests one or two of the other team's pieces that would make it fair.
  • Worth knowing lists things that don't change the verdict, such as a rebuilder paying picks for a running back.
  • Picks are projected early, mid or late from each team's roster strength and record until the draft order is set.
  • Copy link gives a link to this exact trade, to share or to send with feedback.
